# Share scores with members

The public scores page shows every sheet's game, end by end, and the day's
results, on members' phones. No app and no sign-in.
[How it works](../architecture/displays.md#public-scores-page).

1. Open **Displays**, and under **Public Scores Page** tick **Turn on the
   public scores page**.
2. **Copy** the address and share it: in your newsletter, on your website, or
   as a QR code on the warm room wall. **Open** shows it.

Anyone with the address can see it, team names included. Scores reach phones
within about a minute. The page uses the title and logo set for the warm room
screen.

Clear the box to turn it off. Phones that already have the page may see
scores for a few more minutes, until the cached copies run out.
