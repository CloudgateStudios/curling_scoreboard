# Run a bonspiel

Plan the draws and brackets in the portal; the scoreboards offer each sheet
its game, and results move teams on through the brackets by themselves.
[How it works](../architecture/bonspiels.md).

Club admins and bonspiel admins can do all of this. A super admin can make
someone a bonspiel admin under **Settings**, **Users**.

## 1. Create it

On **Bonspiels**, press **+ Add Bonspiel**, name it, **Save**, then
**Open →**. Under **Details** set the dates and **Ends per game**, and tick
**Active: offered on scoreboards during its dates**. A **Short name on the
score bug**, such as "Founders Cup", is optional.

## 2. Add the teams

Under **Teams**, **+ Add Team** for each, or **Paste Teams** to paste a list,
one to a line.

## 3. Fill in the draws

The quickest way is **Use a Template**:

- **Knockout, in one to four events**, and where losers go: four events
  filled by size, by A round, or down one event at a time.
- **Pools played as round robins, then playoffs**, with how many pools and
  how many of each go **To the playoffs**.

Choose the sheets, the **First draw** and the **Minutes between draws**, and
press **Fill In Bonspiel**. Everything it makes can be changed.

Or build it by hand: **+ Add Event** for each event, **+ Add Draw** for each
draw, then on the draw board tap an empty cell to add a game. Each side of a
game is a team, the winner or loser of an earlier game, or a place in a
pool.

Press **Save Changes**. If something doesn't add up, such as a team twice in
a draw, the portal lists what to fix first.

## 4. Play it

On the bonspiel's dates, each connected scoreboard's setup screen offers its
sheet's game in the current draw, from half an hour before it starts, with
the teams filled in as far as they are known. Players press **Start Game**.
If a game moved sheets at the last minute, tapping the left of the bonspiel
bar opens **Which game?**, which lists **Other sheets this draw** too.

When a game finishes, its result reaches the brackets by itself, and the
winner and loser show up in their next games.

To show it in the club, make a display link for the bonspiel; see
[Set up a screen or stream](set-up-a-display.md#a-bonspiel-screen).

## 5. Fix results and ties

Tap a game on the draw board. Under **Result**, choose who won, with the
scores if you like, and press **Done**, then **Save Changes**. Your result
wins over the scoreboard's.

- A **tied** game sends nobody on until you choose who won.
- A game nobody started from the scoreboard can be entered the same way.
- In a pool, **Brackets** shows the standings. Where teams share a place,
  **Move up** puts them in order, after a last stone draw or tiebreak game.

## Next year

**Duplicate** copies the events, draws and games to new dates, without the
teams or results.
